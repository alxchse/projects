09 February 2026

Elios and I met again and worked on how we were going to formalize the states and the Nodes. We began by recognizing that the Nodes will hold a lot more information than the states. The states need to be comparable, but can exist in different nodes. We decided that the states will contain tuples of (row, col, dice) where row and col are coordinates and dice is the current orientation of the dice. This will make engaging with the Dice class we already defined much easier. We decided the nodes will hold state, parent, action (the move taken to get here), gCost, hCost, and fCost. gCost and hCost are necessary to calculate fCost for each node, which will decide how the frontier pqueue is sorted. 

We decided that we were going to use the Manhattan distance heuristic spoken about in class, since it is very simple to calculate and admissible. We then decided to implement a slightly more complicated heuristic. This one will take into account the orientation of the dice and add a penalty of +1 move should there not be a 1 on top. This is because the player will have to rotate the dice at LEAST one more time to not only reach the goal state, but to have a 1 on top as well. Elios made the great point that it would actually be at least TWO moves, but to play it safe, we implemented just +1. The code for these was relatively simple.
